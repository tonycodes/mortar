## Human verification — VoiceOver and keyboard

Not executed. A person with VoiceOver and Full Keyboard Access needs to run this against the AppShell sample on a Mac, and VoiceOver on an iPad and an iPhone. Snapshot tests do not cover it.

### Mac — VoiceOver

1. Open the AppShell sample. Turn VoiceOver on.
2. Each destination announces its title. Items with a count announce the badge value. Drafts (count 0) does not announce a count.
3. Section headers (Projects) are headings, not destinations.
4. The footer account control has a spoken name (`Account, Ada Lovelace`), not an empty name or "image".
5. Changing destination moves to that detail and is announced. Focus can reach the detail.
6. With no brand, VoiceOver does not announce an empty heading.
7. Light and dark: the selected row is still announced.

### Mac — Full Keyboard Access

1. Turn on Full Keyboard Access.
2. Tab or arrow between destinations. Return or Space selects one.
3. The focused row shows Mortar's 2pt focus ring.
4. The footer account control can be reached and activated.
5. After a destination change, keyboard focus can enter the detail and is not trapped in the sidebar.
6. Narrow and wide: focus order stays coherent, and resize does not drop focus.

### iPad — VoiceOver (regular width)

1. Sidebar items and section headers are reachable.
2. The footer account control is named and actionable.
3. Changing destination updates the detail and does not leave focus on the previous screen.

### iPhone — VoiceOver (tab bar)

1. Tabs announce their title, and a badge only when the count is non-zero.
2. Groups are flattened into the tab bar. No destination from the sidebar is missing.
3. Switching tabs does not leave VoiceOver on the previous tab's content.
